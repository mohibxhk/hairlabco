Since 1979

Four decades in the chair.

Long before Hair Lab Co. came to life, our father, Abdul Hameed, laid the foundation in 1979 with Salon Al Qadsiya. What began as a neighborhood salon quickly became a trusted name known for exceptional service, skilled craftsmanship, and a genuine passion for making every client feel at home.

Decades later, that same legacy continues through Hair Lab Co. blending the timeless art of barbering with a modern approach to style, precision, and grooming A legacy started in 1979. A tradition carried forward.
